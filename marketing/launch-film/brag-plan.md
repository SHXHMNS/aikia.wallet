# Brag Plan: aikia.wallet

## What is this app?
A members' card for cafés that lives in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with no app to download. It knows when a guest walks past, remembers their usual, and brings them back on the days the café needs them most. It's a new product from aikia.wrld.

## The angle
**"Every café has regulars. Almost none know who they are."** (the blueprint's own words)
Regulars are *ghosts* to the café they love: faces that come and go without names. aikia.wallet is what makes them visible. The film plays as one day at Café Noor, with morning light, a black-card night reveal, afternoon light and a bright close. Ghosts turn into named members as the product does its job. The ad should feel like a flagship product film: one human detail (a barista's handwriting on a cup), Apple-grade liquid glass, and one idea running through it.

## Hook (0–4.4s)
On a warm Frost (light) canvas, translucent ghost silhouettes drift past with onion-skin echo trails. A paper cup sits in the foreground, and its sleeve has a hand-scrawled "?". The headline lands: **"Every café has regulars."** Then, in handwriting: *"almost none know who they are."*

## Key moments
1. **Halo, in liquid glass.** A real liquid-glass lock-screen notification slides over the scene. Its edges refract the ghosts and stripes behind it: *"Café Noor · 120 m away — Morning, Aarav. Your flat white is on us until 11."* The nearest ghost becomes solid and the "?" on the cup is crossed out. **"Aarav"** is handwritten in its place. Caption: **"It knows when they walk past."**
2. **The black card.** A light-to-dark match cut: the card's black swallows the frame. **"The black card for every café."** The pass (Café Noor, Chrome Member, ₹1,840, Flat white, oat, 6 mornings, stamp row) turns in 3D under a specular sweep. Sub-line: **"Lives in the wallet already on your phone. No app to download."**
3. **Scrubbing the day.** A liquid-glass scrubber knob drags time from 8:42 am to 3:04 pm, with motion-blur ghost frames of the clock digits. The room goes quiet. The owner's thumb taps a pink liquid-glass **Fill My Tables**. Eight ghost members solidify one by one with pink rings. Caption: **"One tap. Every member gets a 45-minute offer."**
4. **Status you can see.** On dark, the card flips Ink → Chrome → Hyper Pink, leaving ghost echoes of each tier behind.

## Outro / punchline
An overexposure bloom resolves to white. A chrome mark spins, then **aikia.wallet** appears with a slow liquid-glass lens gliding across the wordmark and refracting it. **"A members' club. Not a loyalty program."** Below it: *from aikia.wrld*.

## User flow worth showing
Guest walks past → Halo nudge arrives on the lock screen → the café knows them (name and usual). Then the owner, at a quiet hour, taps Fill My Tables → members get the offer. Status climbs Ink → Chrome → Hyper Pink.
