# aikia.wallet — brag plan

**What it is:** A members' card for cafés that lives in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. No app to download.
**Who it's for:** Café, restaurant, bakery and bar owners who don't know who their regulars are.
**What sets it apart:** Halo (the lock screen lights up when a member walks past), Fill My Tables (one button for quiet hours), status tiers you can see (Ink → Chrome → Hyper Pink). Designed and run by a luxury agency, not handed over as a dashboard.
**Most impressive claim:** "It knows when they walk past."
**Visual hook:** A phone on a dark background. A member's dot drifts past a café on a map, a halo ring pulses, and the notification drops: *"Café Noor · 120 m away — Morning, Aarav. Your flat white is on us until 11."*
**Real UI to show:** The blueprint's own wallet pass (Café Noor, Chrome Member, ₹1,840, Flat white, oat, 6 mornings, stamp row), the nudge, the brand mark, Scan → Add → Use.
**Tone:** `polished` with an app-store feel: a luxury members' club launch. Ink and Frost, a single Hyper Pink accent, Dela Gothic One display type.
**Share caption:** The black card for every café. It lives in the wallet already on your guest's phone.

## Angle
Footfall that walked past becomes footfall that walks in. Open on the walk-past moment (the product's signature demo), then reveal the card behind it.

## Sound
A synthesised soft-house bed at 100 BPM in D major: warm pad, sub bass, muted kick, light hats. Effects use notes from the same chord (chime = D6/A5, plucks = chord tones), with gentle reverb-like tails, tucked under the music.
