## 2. What we decided last night (17 Oct)

- **Business:** start with cafés and coffee shops, then grow to 25+ business types. Pricing: **Starter ₹4,999/yr, Growth ₹11,999/yr**, plus a setup fee and messaging margin. Start lean on ₹20–30k by selling setups through the agency.
- **Competitors:** many loyalty players in India (Capillary, EasyRewardz, Reelo and others), but I found no Indian product selling a no-app wallet card with ready presets to small cafés. Abroad, BonusQR and similar sell wallet cards with credit top-ups.
- **The big product idea:** the customer **loads credit** (for example pays ₹2,000, gets ₹2,300), then at the counter staff **scan the wallet QR and deduct the bill**, so there is no payment queue. Friends can **"send a coffee"**: one or two items, only at that café, never money.
- **Legal rules you set:** credit works only at that one café, no cash-out, no money transfer between people. This matches what the RBI has so far treated as a "closed system". The RBI's April 2026 prepaid draft may change this, so **a lawyer's written opinion comes before we build credit.**
- **Screenshot blocking:** not possible on a Google Wallet card or a web page. Instead: a "Pay now" page with a code that changes every 30 seconds, customer approval above about ₹300, and daily caps.
