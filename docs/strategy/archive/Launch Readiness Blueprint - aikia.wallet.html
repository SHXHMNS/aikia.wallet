01

The most important thing

A café is handing us its customers and its reputation. The product survives a missing feature. It does not survive a broken promise. Two promises come before everything else in this document.

Promise 1

Every stamp and every rupee is correct.

A guest who sees 6 stamps when they earned 7, or a balance that is ₹200 short, tells the owner. The owner cancels. The count on the card must always match a record we can show.

Promise 2

One venue's guests are never seen by anyone else.

A member list is a café's most valuable asset. If Venue A can see Venue B's members, or a list leaks online, the business ends that week.

The rule for the team

When a feature and one of these two promises pull in different directions, the promise wins.

02

What we have not discussed yet

Twelve topics that are missing from the earlier blueprints. Each is expanded in the sections that follow.

Fraud

Staff and guests will cheat

Free stamps for friends, shared screenshots of a QR code, fake sign-ups to farm welcome rewards.

Ledger

Points are money

Balances must come from a permanent history of transactions, never from a number someone can overwrite.

Identity

Is the phone number real?

Without a one-time code, anyone can join with a fake number. In India, sending SMS codes needs telecom registration first.

Scale

One button, 2,000 cards

Fill My Tables updates every member at once. That needs a queue, not one long request that times out.

Offline

Café Wi-Fi fails

The scanner needs a fallback for no signal and for a guest whose phone is dead.

Recovery

Lost or new phone

A guest must get their card, stamps and balance back without calling anyone.

Permanent IDs

Choices we can never change

The Apple Pass Type ID and Google Issuer ID are tied to every card ever issued. Changing them orphans all cards.

Exit

When a venue leaves

What happens to guests' cards, unspent prepaid balances and the member list when a café cancels or stops paying.

Our revenue

Collecting our own fee

Automatic monthly billing, GST invoices and a clear process for failed payments.

Visibility

Knowing before the café does

Error tracking, uptime checks and alerts so we find a failure before an owner phones us.

Ownership

Accounts in the company's name

Apple, Google, hosting and domain accounts must sit on company email with two people able to log in.

Proof

Showing the café it works

If the report cannot show extra visits and revenue honestly, venues leave after three months.

05

Fraud and abuse

Assume every reward will be gamed. Owners forgive bugs faster than they forgive paying for free coffee that was never earned.

How people cheatDefencePriority
Staff give stamps to friends or themselvesEvery stamp is tied to a staff login. Owner gets a weekly report of stamps per staff member and an alert on unusual numbers.MUST
Same card scanned again and againA cooldown per member, for example one stamp every two hours, and a daily maximum.MUST
Screenshot of a QR shared in a groupThe cooldown limits the damage. Staff see the member's name on scan. Balance spends above a set amount ask for a PIN.MUST
Fake numbers to farm the welcome rewardVerify the phone number with a one-time code before any reward unlocks. One reward per number.MUST
Referral loopsReferral reward only after the friend's first paid visit, with a monthly cap.SHOULD
Stolen prepaid balanceSpending balance needs a PIN or a tap-to-confirm on the guest's phone. Large top-ups are logged for the owner.MUST
Owner disputes a numberThe audit trail from section 03 lets us show exactly what happened.MUST

Backend: Apple, Google and scale

ItemWhat to know and buildPriority
Permanent identifiersDecide the Apple Pass Type ID, Google Issuer ID and card ID format once. Issue them from the company account. They cannot change after the first real card.MUST
Certificate expiryThe Apple pass certificate expires about once a year. If it lapses, no card can be created or updated. Set reminders 60 and 30 days ahead and write down the renewal steps.MUST
Per-card secretEach Apple card carries its own authentication token. The server must check it on every update request.MUST
Background queueSending an offer to a whole venue runs as queued jobs in small batches with retries, not as one request.MUST
Message caps in codeGoogle allows 3 notifications per card per day. Enforce our own limit of one a day per member in the server so no campaign can exceed it.MUST
Dead devicesWhen Apple reports a device no longer holds the card, remove it from the list so we stop pushing to it.SHOULD
Scheduled jobs that are safe to repeatBirthday and win-back jobs must not send twice if they run twice.MUST
Phones without a walletSome Android phones have no Google Wallet. Give those guests a simple web page version of the card with the same QR.SHOULD
Offline scanningIf the scanner has no signal, let staff look a guest up by phone number later, or queue the scan and send it when the signal returns.SHOULD
Card recoveryA guest re-enters their number, gets a code, and re-adds the same card with history intact.MUST
Brand rulesUse the official Add to Apple Wallet and Add to Google Wallet buttons exactly as their guidelines require. Google reviews this before granting public access.MUST
Load testSimulate 500 sign-ups in an hour and an offer to 2,000 cards before the first busy launch.SHOULD
MonitoringError tracking, an uptime check every minute, and an alert to the team's phones when scans or pushes start failing.MUST

07

Operations and law

Support

When something breaks at 9 am

  • One WhatsApp number for venues, with a promised reply time
  • A short fix-it guide: card not updating, scanner will not open, guest lost their card
  • A manual fallback so the café can keep serving: note the number, add the stamp later
  • A named person on call during café hours
Billing

Getting paid automatically

  • Monthly auto-debit through a payment provider
  • GST invoice sent each month
  • Failed payment: reminder, grace period, then pause new sign-ups before anything harsher
  • Never switch off guests' existing rewards over a late bill without warning
Contract with venues

What the agreement must say

  • The venue owns its member list and can export it
  • aikia processes that data only to run the service
  • What happens to cards and data on cancellation
  • Prepaid money is collected and owed by the venue, not by aikia
  • Service level, liability limits and notice period
Venue exit

Leaving cleanly

  • Guests get a final message and a date
  • Unspent prepaid balance is the venue's duty to honour or refund
  • The venue receives its data export
  • Cards are marked expired, then data is deleted after the agreed period
Legal: confirm with a lawyer

Privacy law

  • Clear consent notice at sign-up, in plain words
  • A way to withdraw consent and delete data
  • A named contact for complaints
  • India's data law treats under-18s as children who need a parent's consent. Decide whether to add an age question.
  • A written plan for what we do in the first 72 hours of a data leak
Legal: confirm with a CA

Money and messages

  • Prepaid balances stay tied to one venue. Check the rules before switching this feature on.
  • How GST applies to prepaid top-ups and free rewards
  • SMS one-time codes in India need sender and template registration with the telecom operators. WhatsApp codes are an alternative.
  • Marketing messages only to guests who agreed to them
Company hygiene

Nothing depends on one person

  • All accounts on company email, with two admins
  • Recovery codes stored in a shared password manager
  • A one-page map of every service, who owns it and when it renews
  • Check the name aikia.wallet for trademark conflicts
Proof of value

The monthly report

  • Members joined, visits, repeat rate, rewards redeemed
  • Visits that followed an offer or a Halo nudge
  • Revenue only where staff entered the bill amount. Never estimate it and present it as fact.
  • Removal rate of cards, as an early warning
