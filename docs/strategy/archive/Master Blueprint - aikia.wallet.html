01

The idea in one minute

Every café has regulars, and almost none of them know who those regulars are. Paper stamp cards get lost. Loyalty apps never get downloaded. Delivery platforms keep the customer's number and charge commission on every order.

aikia.wallet gives each venue its own members' card inside Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. A guest scans one QR code at the counter and has the card in ten seconds. From then on the venue can recognise them, reward them, hold their prepaid balance and message their lock screen for free.

The positioning

We do not sell a loyalty program. We sell a members' club, designed by a luxury agency and run for the owner.

Software companies hand a café a dashboard and leave. aikia designs the card, writes the offers, runs the campaigns and reports the extra revenue. That service layer is what competitors cannot copy.

02

The problem we solve for owners

Pain 1

They do not own their customers

Aggregators and payment apps hold the phone number. The café cannot reach its own regulars without paying someone.

Pain 2

Empty hours cost money

Rent and staff are fixed. A quiet Tuesday at 3 pm is pure loss, and the owner has no button to press to fill it.

Pain 3

Discounts go to strangers

Blanket offers attract one-time deal hunters. Regulars who would have come anyway get the same discount.

Pain 4

Nobody downloads café apps

A single-venue app is expensive to build and guests will not install it. The wallet is already on every phone.

04

Signature features

Four headline features carry the pitch. The rest make the product hard to leave.

I

Halo: the walk-past nudge

When a member comes within a set distance of the café, their lock screen lights up with a personal offer. This is your original idea, and it becomes the demo that closes the sale.

  • Works on iPhone and Android. The phone decides the exact distance (roughly 100 m), and each card can watch up to 10 spots.
  • Smart rules: the nudge is switched off outside opening hours and for the rest of the day after a visit.
  • The message changes with context: morning coffee, a rainy-day hot chocolate, a slow-afternoon treat.

Why it wins: it turns footfall that was walking past into footfall that walks in, at zero cost per message.

II

Fill My Tables: the quiet-hour button

The owner taps one button when the room is empty. Every member of that café gets a 45-minute offer on their phone. When the room fills, the offer ends by itself.

  • Offers go only to members, so the public menu price is never discounted.
  • The dashboard shows how many came and what they spent.

Why it wins: airlines and hotels price by demand. No independent café can do it today.

III

Status you can see: Ink, Chrome, Pink

The card itself changes as a guest climbs. It starts as Ink, turns Chrome, then Hyper Pink for the top few percent. People chase status harder than they chase points.

  • Each tier unlocks things money cannot buy: a secret menu, a reserved table, first taste of new items.
  • Staff see the tier and the guest's name on scan and greet them by it.

Why it wins: a free coffee is forgotten. Being a Pink member at your café is talked about.

IV

The Circle: one city, one passport

Every venue on aikia.wallet joins a curated network. A member of one café discovers the others, and their status is recognised across the Circle.

  • Venues send each other guests instead of paying for ads.
  • City challenges: visit five Circle venues this month and unlock a reward.
  • Balances stay with each venue. Only status and discovery travel.

Why it wins: each new venue makes the card more valuable for every other venue. This is how the product becomes a network and not a tool.

06

What we need to build and run it

Technology

The stack

  • Apple Developer account and a Pass Type ID certificate
  • Google Wallet issuer account
  • Pass engine: creates, updates and pushes to cards
  • Staff scanner as a web app for any phone or tablet
  • Owner dashboard and campaign builder
  • WhatsApp Business API for joins, gifts and receipts
  • Later: links to common POS systems
Fastest route

Rent first, build second

  • Phase 1: launch on a white-label wallet-pass platform under our brand. Live in weeks, low cost, proves demand.
  • Phase 2: once 25 or more venues pay, build our own engine for margin and for Circle features nobody else has.

This avoids spending months building before a single café has paid.

How we sell it

The 30-second pitch

"How many of your regulars can you message right now? With us, every guest carries your card in their phone wallet. When they walk past, their phone tells them to come in. When your café is empty, you press one button and fill it. We design it, we run it, and you pay a flat fee with no commission."

The demo

Make them feel it

  • Owner scans our QR and adds a demo card with their café's name on it.
  • We send a live notification to their phone while they watch.
  • We show the dashboard with sample members and revenue.

The card on their own lock screen sells better than any slide.

The offer

Founding 10

  • First 10 venues get setup free and 60 days at no charge.
  • In return: a case study, a video testimonial and two referrals.
  • Founding venues keep a locked price for life.
Who we approach first

Targets

  • Independent specialty cafés with strong repeat traffic
  • Premium bakeries and dessert bars
  • Venues near offices, colleges and gyms
  • Existing aikia.wrld clients and their networks
  • Small groups with 2 to 5 outlets
Channels

How we reach them

  • Walk in during quiet hours and ask for the owner
  • Instagram DMs with a mock-up of their card
  • Referrals between Circle venues
  • Partnerships with POS resellers and café suppliers
  • A launch event for founding venues

Likely objections

"We already have a stamp card."

Keep the idea, lose the paper. Ours cannot be lost, tells you who the guest is and lets you message them.

"My staff are too busy."

Staff say one sentence and scan one code. We train them and the scan takes two seconds.

"Will guests find it annoying?"

Messages are capped at one a day and only sent when relevant. Guests can remove the card in one tap.

"It is another monthly cost."

It pays for itself at roughly 30 extra visits a month. Founding venues pay nothing for 60 days.

11

Rules and risks to handle early

AreaWhat to knowWhat we do
Nearby alerts designBoth Apple Wallet and Google Wallet support nearby alerts, up to 10 locations per card. The alert is a quiet lock-screen card, not a loud buzz, and the phone controls the distance.Test on both phones in the pilot. Pair Halo with normal push messages for offers that must be noticed.
Prepaid balances legal checkStored value that works only at the issuing café is treated more lightly in India than a wallet usable across merchants.Keep every balance tied to one venue. Confirm with a CA or lawyer before launch. No cross-venue money.
Privacy and consent legal checkWe hold names, phone numbers and visit history. India's data protection law requires clear consent and a way to withdraw.Plain consent at sign-up, one-tap opt-out, no selling of data, venue owns its member list.
Notification fatigue designToo many messages and guests delete the card.Hard cap of one nudge a day. Track removal rate per venue.
Staff adoption designIf staff do not offer the card, nobody joins.One-line script, a small staff reward per sign-up, weekly leaderboard.
Copycats strategyThe pass itself is easy to copy.Win on design, service and the Circle network, which grows stronger with each venue.
