Part AGet ready
01
30 minutes

Gather your materials

  1. A company email address for all accounts, for example one at aikia.world.
  2. The aikia logo as a PNG with a clear background.
  3. A wide banner image for the top of the card. Apple's size for this strip is 1125 × 369 pixels.
  4. The brand colours: Ink #14111F, Hyper Pink #FF3D9A, Frost #F8F9FD.
  5. A debit or credit card that works for international payments.
  6. One iPhone and one Android phone for testing.
Done when: all six are in one folder or on your desk.
02
20 minutes, then 1 to 2 days waiting

Join the Apple Developer Program

  1. Go to developer.apple.com/programs and choose to enrol.
  2. Sign in with an Apple ID that uses your company email. Turn on two-step verification if asked.
  3. Enrol as an individual to start. It is the fastest option.
  4. Pay the US$99 yearly fee.
  5. Wait for Apple's approval email.
Do this first. It is the only step with a waiting time, and PassKit cannot issue real cards without it.
Done when: you can log in at developer.apple.com and see Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles.
03
10 minutes

Create your PassKit account

  1. Go to passkit.com and start the free trial with the company email.
  2. Open the email PassKit sends and confirm your address.
  3. Log in to the portal at app.passkit.com.
Done when: you can see the PassKit portal home screen.
04
20 minutes, after Apple approves

Connect Apple to PassKit

  1. In PassKit, open the Certificates section and click ADD CERTIFICATE. A file called cert.certSigningRequest downloads. PassKit made it for you.
  2. In your Apple Developer account, open Identifiers and click the plus button.
  3. Choose Pass Type IDs and click Continue.
  4. Type a description such as "aikia wallet" and an identifier such as pass.world.aikia.wallet. Click Register.
  5. Open the new Pass Type ID and click Create Certificate.
  6. Click Choose File, pick the file from step 1, click Continue, and download pass.cer.
  7. Back in PassKit's Certificates section, click ADD CERTIFICATE, choose I ALREADY HAVE A CSR, and drag pass.cer into the box.
The identifier can never be changed later without every guest re-adding their card. Choose it once and write it down.
Done when: the certificate shows in PassKit's list and VIEW opens its details.
Part BMake the card
05
10 minutes

Create the demo project

  1. In the PassKit portal, create a new project.
  2. Choose the membership or loyalty type.
  3. Name it "aikia demo". It starts as a draft, which is PassKit's test mode.
Done when: the project opens and shows a blank card design.
06
45 minutes

Design the card

  1. Open the design screen. There is one design for Apple and one for Google. Do both.
  2. Upload the logo and the banner image.
  3. Set the background to Ink, the text to Frost and labels to Hyper Pink.
  4. Set the name shown on the card to "Your Café". You will rename it before each meeting.
  5. Keep the barcode type as QR code.
Done when: the preview looks like the sample card in the Master Blueprint.
07
30 minutes

Add the fields guests will see

  1. Member name: the guest's name from the sign-up form.
  2. Stamps: use the points field and label it "Stamps".
  3. Tier: shows Ink, Chrome or Pink.
  4. Offer: a text field the same for everyone, starting with "Welcome to the club". This becomes the Fill My Tables message.
  5. The usual: an optional field for the guest's regular order.
  6. On the back of the card add opening hours, address and the line "Powered by aikia.wallet".
Done when: all five fields appear on the preview.
08
15 minutes

Switch on lock-screen messages

  1. Go to Design, then Apple Design.
  2. Click the Stamps field. In Apple Lock Screen Message type: You now have %@ stamps
  3. Click the Offer field. In its lock-screen message type: %@
  4. Save.
The %@ symbol stands for the new value. Without it no message appears, even though the card updates.
Done when: both fields show a lock-screen message containing %@.
09
20 minutes

Create the three tiers

  1. Add three tiers to the project: Ink, Chrome and Pink.
  2. Give each its own colours. Ink is dark, Chrome is silver-grey, Pink uses Hyper Pink.
  3. Make Ink the tier new members start on.
Done when: switching tier in the preview changes how the card looks.
10
10 minutes

Add the Halo nearby nudge

  1. Go to Designs, then Settings, then Location.
  2. Click Add Location.
  3. Enter a name and an address. For testing, use your own home or office.
  4. Type a short lock-screen message, for example "Your coffee is 2 minutes away. Come in."
  5. Save.
Done when: the location appears in the list. You can add up to 10 per card.
11
15 minutes

Set up the sign-up page

  1. Open the project's distribution or sign-up settings.
  2. Keep the form short: name and phone number. Add birthday only if you will use it.
  3. Add a consent line: "I agree to receive offers from this café. I can remove the card at any time."
  4. Copy the sign-up link and download its QR code.
Done when: opening the link on a phone shows your form.
Part CGo live and test
12
20 minutes

Test the draft on both phones

  1. Scan the QR with the iPhone, fill in the form and tap Add to Apple Wallet.
  2. Do the same on the Android phone with Add to Google Wallet.
  3. Check the colours, logo and every field on both.
  4. Fix anything in the design and check again.
Draft cards are deleted automatically after 48 hours, and a new account can issue only a few. They are for checking the design, not for demos.
Done when: the card looks right on both phones.
13
10 minutes

Set the project live

  1. Add a payment method in your PassKit account. You are not charged during the trial.
  2. From the project's Overview page or the Projects list, find the launch option.
  3. Select your Apple certificate and click Launch.
  4. The live project starts empty. Copy its new sign-up link and QR code. The draft ones no longer apply.
  5. Add the live card to both phones again.
Done when: both phones hold a card from the live project and it does not disappear after two days.
14
45 minutes

Set up the scanner and run a full test

  1. In the portal's app settings, switch on the actions staff need: adding points and redeeming.
  2. On a phone, install PassKit PassReader from the App Store or Google Play.
  3. Sign in with your PassKit username and select the aikia demo programme.
  4. Scan the card on the other phone and add 1 stamp. Watch for "You now have 1 stamps" on the iPhone.
  5. In the portal, open Members, pick your test member and move them to Chrome. Check the card changes.
  6. Change the Offer field's default value to "Free cookie with any coffee until 5 pm" and save. Every iPhone card should show it on the lock screen.
  7. Walk away from the test address and come back. Check whether the card appears on each lock screen.
Done when: stamp, tier change, offer and nearby nudge have all worked at least once. Write down anything that did not work on Android.
Part DBuild your demo kit
15
1 hour

Make the physical kit

  1. Print the live QR code on a small counter stand with the line "Scan to join the club".
  2. Keep PassReader logged in on your second phone.
  3. Print the pricing page and the 30-second pitch from the Master Blueprint.
  4. Save a screenshot of each tier design to show the Ink, Chrome and Pink cards side by side.
Done when: everything fits in one folder you can carry into a café.
16
5 minutes before each meeting

Personalise the demo

  1. In the design, change "Your Café" to the venue's real name.
  2. Change the location to the venue's address and the Halo message to mention them.
  3. Add the card to your own phone again to check it.
Done when: the card on your phone carries their name.
17
Practise until it takes 3 minutes

Rehearse the live demo

  1. Hand the owner the QR stand: "Scan this with your camera."
  2. They fill in two fields and add the card. Say: "That took ten seconds and no app."
  3. Scan their card with PassReader and add a stamp. Their phone shows the new count.
  4. Change the Offer in the portal. Their lock screen lights up with it.
  5. Show the member list in the portal: "Every guest who joins appears here, and they are yours."
  6. Show the three tier designs, then the price page.
Done when: you have run the demo on a friend without looking at notes.
What to say while their phone lights up

"That message just reached you for free. Imagine it going to 600 of your regulars on a quiet Tuesday afternoon."

Part EYour first venue says yes
18
At the meeting

Collect the venue sheet

  1. Logo as a PNG and two or three brand colours.
  2. One wide photo for the card banner.
  3. Full address and opening hours.
  4. Reward rule, for example 9 stamps = 1 free coffee.
  5. When a guest becomes Chrome and Pink, for example 15 and 40 visits.
  6. The Halo message and a welcome offer.
  7. Which staff will scan.
Done when: the signed agreement and the sheet are both in your folder.
19
1 to 2 hours

Build the venue's card

  1. Create a new project named after the venue. Copy the aikia demo design as the starting point if PassKit offers a duplicate option.
  2. Apply their logo, colours and banner to all three tiers.
  3. Repeat steps 7 to 11 with their fields, location and sign-up form.
  4. Test the draft, then launch it with the same Apple certificate as in step 13.
  5. Add the live card to both of your phones and run the full test from step 14.
Done when: their live card works end to end on both phones.
20
Half a day at the venue

Launch day at the café

  1. Create a PassKit login for the venue's scanning device and install PassReader on it. Each login adds to your PassKit bill.
  2. Place the QR stand at the counter and tents on tables.
  3. Teach staff one sentence: "Would you like our members' card? It takes ten seconds."
  4. Do three practice scans with each staff member.
  5. Stay for the first hour and sign up the first guests yourself.
Done when: the venue has its first 20 real members.
21
30 minutes a week per venue

Run it by hand for the pilot

  1. Tier upgrades: once a week, sort members by stamps and move those who qualify.
  2. Fill My Tables: when the owner messages you, change the Offer field. Set a 45-minute timer and change it back.
  3. Report: send the owner member count, scans and rewards redeemed every Monday.
  4. Limit: no more than one offer a day per venue.
Done when: two weeks pass with correct counts and a happy owner.
Part FAdd our own layer, once venues pay

Parts A to E need no code. This part does, and it is what turns a PassKit account into the aikia.wallet product. Do it after one to three venues are paying. The full plan is in the PassKit Route Blueprint.

22
About 1 week with a developer or AI coding help

Our own join page and web scanner

  1. A join page on wallet.aikia.world styled per venue.
  2. A scanner web page staff open on any phone with a PIN, so venues no longer need PassKit logins or see the PassKit name.
  3. Our own record of every stamp.
Done when: a full visit happens without anyone opening a PassKit screen.
23
About 1 more week

Owner dashboard and automation

  1. A dashboard for owners with a real Fill My Tables button that expires by itself.
  2. Automatic tier upgrades, birthday treats and win-back offers.
  3. Scan cooldowns and staff logs to stop cheating.
Done when: step 21 no longer needs you.
24
Before selling widely

Pass the launch gates

  1. Work through the 17 gates in the Launch Readiness Blueprint.
  2. Get the venue contract, privacy text and prepaid balance rules checked by a lawyer and a CA.
  3. Ask PassKit in writing whether its terms allow reselling under your brand.
Done when: every gate is ticked. Now sell to as many venues as you can serve.

Limits to know before you promise anything

TopicWhat is trueWhat to do
Android lock-screen messagesPassKit's help says Android cards update but do not show the lock-screen message that iPhones do.In demos, show the message on an iPhone. Tell owners honestly that Android guests see the offer when they open the card.
Nearby nudge on AndroidPassKit's help describes the nearby message for Apple devices. Google added nearby alerts in 2025, but I could not confirm PassKit switches them on.Test it in step 14 and ask PassKit support.
Nearby distanceThe phone decides how close is "near". The alert is a quiet lock-screen card, not a buzz.Say "when they are near", never a number of metres.
Scanner logins cost moneyPassReader needs a PassKit login, and logins set your monthly fee.One device per venue during the pilot. Build our own scanner in step 22.
Offers reach everyoneChanging the Offer field reaches all members of that venue, not only those nearby.Keep to one offer a day.
The PassKit nameThe scanner app and portal always show PassKit.Fine for a pilot. Step 22 removes it from the venue's view.
Prepaid balanceNot part of this draft.Add only after the legal check in step 24.
