The decision. Basic Rs 499 a month (or Rs 4,999 a year), Plus Rs 799 a month (or Rs 7,999 a year), no setup fee for the first 50 founding cafes. Prices before GST. Break-even at about 45 cafes; all money back by month 17 at 12 new cafes a month.
Why not cheaper. At Rs 299 we would need 158 cafes just to cover running costs. For a brand nobody knows yet, that is a year of selling before we stop losing money. Rs 499 is cheap enough to feel like no risk and still reachable.

Reading the ladder

  • Rs 299 is the cheapest price that covers anything, but needs 158 cafes before we stop losing money. Too slow for a new brand.
  • Rs 499 is the sweet spot: Rs 17 a day for the cafe, and 58 cafes to cover running costs.
  • Rs 999 and up breaks even faster, but loses the "too cheap to say no" message we need without references.
  • Every price below about Rs 186 loses money on each cafe, whatever the number of cafes.

Rules from the finance desk

  1. Sell yearly first. Offer Rs 4,999 a year with two months free. Ten yearly cafes bring Rs 50,000 on day one, which funds the launch without borrowing.
  2. Never go below Rs 186 a month. Below that, each cafe costs more than it pays.
  3. No discounts on top of the founding offer. The price is already the discount.
  4. Keep 3 months of running cost (Rs 53,172) in the bank at all times.
  5. Watch two numbers every month: cafes leaving (keep under 3%) and new cafes (keep at or above 12).
  6. Raise prices only for new cafes, once we have 3 case studies and 50+ cafes. Founding cafes keep theirs.
  7. Build the remaining work ourselves where we can: every Rs 1 lakh saved on development is about 20 fewer cafes needed in year one.
