Finance desk verdict. Rs 899 / Rs 1,199 is the stronger business: it breaks even with half the cafes, needs less cash in hand and earns about twice the profit per cafe. It still sits far below every Indian competitor we found. The risk is a slower yes from cafes, so it needs a strong founding offer.
How to read this. Planning numbers, not quotes. The average price assumes 30% of cafes take Plus. Scenario B assumes cafes sign up 25% slower than at Rs 499 (9 a month instead of 12). Prices are before GST; cafes pay 18% GST on top if we are registered.

What the research says about Indian buyers

  • Indian businesses pay 50 to 70%% less than US and EU customers for the same software, and traditional small businesses are the most price-sensitive.
  • Negotiation is expected. Practitioners plan for a 20 to 30%% discount request and set the list price above the target. Rs 899 leaves room to "give" a founding discount and still land near Rs 629 to 719.
  • They pay for immediate, visible value. A card in the customer's phone wallet and repeat visits the owner can count are easier to justify than a dashboard.
Position. Even at Rs 899 we are about 72%% below Reelo, at the bottom of the global wallet-loyalty range, and the only option with Google and Apple Wallet cards built in. No formal Indian willingness-to-pay survey for this category was found; our first 20 sales calls are the real test.

6  /  RECOMMENDATION

How to sell Rs 899 so cafes still say yes

PlanList a monthYearly (2 months free)Founding offer, first 50 cafes
Basic: wallet card, stamps, tiers, offers, geofencing, staff scanner, unlimited customersRs 899Rs 8,999Rs 6,999 for the first year
Plus: Basic + credit and scan-and-charge, Send-a-gift, weather messages, money reportRs 1,199Rs 11,999Rs 8,999 for the first year
  1. Lead with the yearly price. "Rs 8,999 a year, Rs 25 a day" is easier than "Rs 899 a month", and brings cash in on day one.
  2. The founding offer is the negotiation room. Indian buyers expect 20 to 30% off; the founding price gives exactly that once, in writing, for the first 50 cafes only.
  3. 30-day free trial with the card live in the cafe's customers' phones. The trial does the selling.
  4. Show the maths to the owner: 2 extra repeat visits a day at Rs 150 pays for Basic in 3 days.
  5. Keep Rs 499 in the pocket. If fewer than 1 in 8 pitched cafes sign after 2 months, switch new cafes to the Rs 499 plan (section 3 shows it still works).
  6. Never go below the founding price (Rs 6,999 a year, about Rs 583 a month, on Basic), and never give it after the first 50 cafes.
  7. Same money rules as before: 3 months of running cost in the bank, churn under 3%, at least 9 new cafes a month.
Founding offer check. If all 50 founding cafes pay the founding yearly prices (average about Rs 7,599 a year, about Rs 633 a month), break-even rises to about 40 cafes during the founding year. The cafes after 50 pay list price.
