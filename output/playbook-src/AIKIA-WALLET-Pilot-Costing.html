AIKIA.WALLET  /  PILOT COSTING

What 3 to 5 cafes
cost us to run.

Monthly and yearly running costs for 3 to 5 cafes with 100 to 200 customers each, the cost per cafe, one-time costs, the development still to do, break-even pricing and a roadmap. Prices checked 19 October 2026 at Rs 96 to the US dollar, plus 18% GST on imported software.

Rs 17,311A MONTH to run 3 cafes with 150 customers each (450 cards), all-in.
Rs 18,841A MONTH to run 5 cafes with 150 customers each (750 cards). That is Rs 3,768 a cafe.
Rs 2,26,088A YEAR for 5 cafes, plus Rs 33,000 to Rs 72,000 one-time to start.
The big finding. At this size most of the cost is fixed platform fees, not customers. PassKit also charges about Rs 5 a customer card every month after the first 250. At our earlier Starter price of Rs 4,999 a year (Rs 417 a month) a cafe never covers its own cost. Pilot pricing must be closer to Rs 1,500 to Rs 2,000 a month, or we move to our own Google Wallet engine early.
How to read this. Figures are planning numbers, not quotes. S = price found in a published source (re-check on the vendor page before paying). E = our estimate. Founder time is not costed. Development is shown separately because most of the product is already built.

What this means for the pilot

  • The pilot runs at a loss, and that is normal. With 5 cafes at Rs 1,499 a month we collect Rs 7,495 and spend about Rs 18,841: a gap of about Rs 11,346 a month. That gap is the price of proof.
  • Charge a one-time setup fee. Rs 2,999 to Rs 4,999 per cafe covers the standee, the onboarding visit and part of the legal set-up.
  • Suggested pilot offer: setup Rs 2,999, then Rs 1,499 a month (or Rs 14,999 a year paid upfront), with credit and gifts as a Growth add-on once the lawyer approves.
  • Move to our own Google engine as soon as Google approves. It removes the biggest fixed fee and the per-card fee, so about 8 to 10 cafes at Rs 1,499 cover the bill.
Re-price the old plans. The earlier Rs 4,999-a-year Starter plan does not cover cost at any number of cafes on PassKit. Keep it only for the own-engine set-up and only at 25+ cafes.

Risks that change the cost

  • PassKit draft mode. Until the Apple certificate is uploaded, cards are deleted after about 48 hours. Do not onboard real customers before this.
  • Nearby (geofence) messages. On PassKit, location alerts work on Apple Wallet only. On Android, weather offers still reach guests as card messages, but not as "you are near the cafe" alerts.
  • Dollar rate. Most costs are in US dollars. Every Rs 5 move in the rate changes the fixed bill by about Rs 718 a month.
  • Cards that are never deleted. PassKit bills every stored card. Clean up members who leave, every month.
