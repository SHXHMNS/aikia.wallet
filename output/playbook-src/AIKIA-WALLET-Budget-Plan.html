Rule of thumb for pricing

  • A venue must pay at least 3x its monthly cost to serve, or support time will erase the margin.
  • Small venues only work if onboarding is self-serve. Spending a person-day on a Rs 1,499 plan loses money for the first year.
  • Chains and institutions should pay setup fees that cover their integration and security work.
7 / DEVELOPMENT PARTNER

Working with your development company

A 20-year relationship helps, but the product, the code and the customer data are the company's most valuable assets, so the paper must be as clear as with a stranger.

Suggested payment structure (fixed-scope packages)

MilestoneShare of packageCondition
Start15%Signed scope, person-month estimate, schedule, IP assignment, NDA
Design and architecture sign-off20%Written design for the package reviewed by you or an independent reviewer
Working beta in a pilot venue30%Demo on a real phone and counter, with test results
Production acceptance25%Acceptance tests pass; security fixes closed; documentation delivered
Warranty holdback10%Paid 60 days after go-live if no serious defects

Alternative: time-and-materials with a monthly cap, weekly demos and a written burn report. Use this for the exploratory work (integrations) and fixed-scope for the well-defined work (billing, rules, scanner).

Put in the contract

  • All code and designs assigned to the company; code lives in the company's own GitHub organisation from day one
  • No partner-owned libraries without an open licence; list of third-party components
  • Security duties: no secrets in code, access by named people, breach notice within hours
  • Data ownership: member data belongs to the venue; partner is a sub-processor with a data-processing agreement
  • Support terms after go-live: response times, upkeep retainer, warranty
  • Non-solicit and confidentiality; key-person cover so one engineer leaving does not stop delivery

Controls on your side

  • One internal owner who accepts or rejects each package
  • Weekly demo; monthly budget review against this plan
  • Independent code review once per quarter (a few person-days, about Rs 40,000 to 80,000)
  • Change requests priced in person-months before work starts
  • Keep the build and deployment accounts in the company's name, not the partner's
  • Do not pre-pay beyond the milestone table
If the partner offers equity or revenue share instead of cash: treat it as a separate legal decision with its own lawyer. It lowers early cash but gives a third party a claim on the company, and it changes who decides the roadmap.
