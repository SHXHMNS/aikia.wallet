AIKIA.WALLET  /  COST PER CAFE

What one cafe
costs us to serve.

Cost price only, no selling price. Every item we need to run the service and look after a cafe, per month and per year. Own Google and Apple wallet, no PassKit. Prices checked 19 October 2026 at Rs 96 to the US dollar.

Rs 7,136A MONTH per cafe if we serve 5 cafes (year 1, including start-up costs). Rs 85,636 a year.
Rs 3,645A MONTH per cafe at 5 cafes from year 2 (start-up costs repaid). Rs 43,736 a year.
Rs 100A MONTH: the cafe's own direct cost (support, hosting growth, extras). The rest is its share of shared costs.
Why the number depends on how many cafes we have. Most of the cost is shared: hosting, database, Apple, accounts and travel cost the same whether we serve 1 cafe or 50. A cafe's cost is its direct cost plus its share of the shared bill. Section 3 shows it for 1 to 100 cafes.
Not included. Founders' salaries and time, office rent, paid advertising, WhatsApp or SMS messages, a penetration test (about Rs 1 to 1.5 lakh once, past 10 cafes) and the lawyer's opinion on prepaid credit (Rs 50,000 to 1 lakh, only before real-money credit).
