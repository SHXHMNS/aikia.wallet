Is one fixed Rs 1,000 price right for India?

One flat plan is easy to explain, sell and forecast, but gives up upsell revenue. Companies that moved from tiers to one plan did it for higher conversion.Right for launch: one sentence pitch, one invoice, one price on the poster. Add-ons can come later.S
Indian small businesses compare against the cheapest option and usually ask for special pricing.A written fixed price with a public "same price for everyone" rule ends the haggle. The answer to a discount request is the yearly plan, not a lower price.S

  • Our choice: registered, "Rs 1,000 + GST" (the middle row). We keep the full Rs 1,000, give every cafe a proper GST invoice, and claim back the GST on our imported software (about Rs 1,560 a month).
  • The cafe pays Rs 1,180 a month or Rs 14,160 a year. GST-registered cafes claim the Rs 180 back as input credit. Small cafes on the composition scheme, or not registered, can't, so for them the price is really Rs 1,180. Say it upfront.
  • Never let it slip to "Rs 1,000 including GST": that quietly cuts Rs 153 from every cafe every month.
  • Below Rs 20 lakh a year (about 167 cafes) registration is voluntary. Registering from day one is the cleaner, more trusted choice; it needs monthly GST returns, which the CA retainer already covers.
Ask the CA. GST registration before the first invoice, the invoice format, the place-of-supply rules if we sell outside Gujarat, and whether cafes will want a GST invoice. These figures are a briefing, not tax advice.

Rules for a fixed price

  1. One price, written everywhere: "Rs 1,000 a month + GST" on the poster, website, proposal and invoice. "Same price for every cafe."
  2. No discounts, ever. If a cafe asks, the only answer is the yearly plan and the 30-day free trial.
  3. Yearly first: Rs 12,000 + GST (Rs 14,160) upfront by UPI or bank transfer. Monthly Rs 1,180 by UPI Autopay.
  4. 30-day free trial with the card live; the trial does the convincing, not the price.
  5. Price lock for the first 50 cafes for 2 years. This is a promise, not a discount, and costs us nothing now.
  6. Same money rules: 3 months of running cost in the bank (Rs 48,492), churn under 3%, at least 9 new cafes a month.
  7. Review after 3 months: if fewer than 1 in 8 pitched cafes sign, test the Rs 899 / Rs 1,199 plan on new cafes; never cut existing ones.
