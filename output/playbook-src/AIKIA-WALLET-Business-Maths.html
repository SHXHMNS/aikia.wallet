Three numbers
run this business.

We build everything ourselves and already own the phones and laptops. So the only real cost is the online software. This page shows why we can't go bankrupt, how many cafes we need, and when the money becomes good. Price: Rs 1,500 a month + GST per cafe.

Rs 8,664NUMBER 1: OUR MONTHLY BILL. All the software, system and security. The same for 1 cafe or 150.
Rs 1,485NUMBER 2: WHAT WE KEEP FROM EACH CAFE a month (Rs 1,500 minus about 1% payment fee; the GST goes to the government).
6 cafesNUMBER 3: BREAK-EVEN. Bill divided by what each cafe pays us. From cafe 6, every cafe is profit.
The whole business in one line. Profit a month = (number of cafes x Rs 1,485) minus Rs 8,664. That's it.
Why we can't go bankrupt. Our worst case is zero cafes for a whole year. That costs Rs 1,03,968 a year: the software bill. Keep that amount aside and nothing else can sink us. There is no rent, no salary bill and no loan in this model.

Customers cost nothing. Google and Apple charge nothing per wallet card. A cafe with 100 customers and a cafe with 500 cost us the same. More cafes don't raise the bill until about 150 cafes, when the database needs an upgrade (about Rs 6,000 more a month).
Left out, as you asked: development (we build it), phones and laptops (we own them), lawyer and CA fees, travel and physical work. GST on the software is left out too, because as a GST-registered business we claim it back.

Until the first cafe pays, we can demo on the free Vercel and Supabase plans and pay only Apple (about Rs 792 a month). Switch to the paid plans the day the first cafe pays.

4  /  THE RULES

Five rules so we never go into loss

  1. Keep Rs 1,03,968 in the bank before launch: one full year of the software bill. With it, even zero sales can't hurt us.
  2. Get 6 paying cafes first. Until then, stay on free plans for demos.
  3. One price, no discounts: Rs 1,500 + GST a month, or Rs 18,000 + GST a year.
  4. Ask for yearly payment first. One yearly cafe pays the software bill for about 2 months.
  5. Watch one number every month: cafes. If it goes up, profit goes up. If cafes leave, find out why that same week.
Check the bill every 6 months. Prices are in US dollars; if the rupee weakens by Rs 5, the bill rises by about Rs 451 a month. Still tiny next to what 74 cafes bring in.
