Our recommendation

  • Rs 2,999 a month per cafe (Rs 29,999 a year upfront, two months free), all features included. That is about Rs 100 a day for the cafe.
  • Rs 4,999 one-time setup per cafe: card design, tiers, staff training, standee. Every 5 paid setups (Rs 24,995) cover most of the one-time legal and set-up costs.
  • Founding-cafe offer for the first 3: setup free, Rs 2,999 a month, in return for a case study. The gap of about Rs 3,939 a month closes when cafe 5 joins.
  • Above 5 cafes everything is surplus: each new cafe adds about Rs 2,928 a month after fees, because running cost doesn't grow at this size.

How we avoid lag and breakdowns

  • The counter never waits on Google or Apple. The ledger is saved first. The wallet card updates right after, and if Google or Apple is slow, the daily retry finishes the job.
  • No double charges. Every scan carries a one-time key, so a double tap or a weak network can't charge twice.
  • Fast for India. Database in Mumbai; app served from Vercel's edge network.
  • Safe changes. Staging first, automatic tests, preview link, then live. Roll back in one click.
  • Room to grow. Vercel Pro and Supabase Pro comfortably carry 5 to 50 cafes before any upgrade.
