Reading the ladder

  • At Rs 1,000 the founders' Rs 1 lakh needs 145 cafes: more than a new brand reaches in 12 months at our selling speed.
  • At Rs 1,500 it needs 90 cafes, which 9 new cafes a month reaches by about month 11 or 12.
  • At Rs 2,000 it needs 65 cafes, but the price starts to feel "costly" against a Rs 2 to 5 lakh cafe, and sign-ups slow.
  • Above 150 cafes the costs step up (database upgrade and a part-time support person, about Rs 21,000 a month), which is why the Rs 1 lakh-each column jumps.

5  /  RECOMMENDATION

The price and how to hold it

ItemDecision
PriceRs 1,500 a month + GST (cafe pays Rs 1,770); Rs 18,000 a year + GST (Rs 21,240). One price, every feature, every cafe.
No negotiationThe same price is printed on the poster, website, proposal and invoice. The only "offer" is the 30-day free trial.
Founding promiseThe first 50 cafes keep Rs 1,500 for 2 years, even if the price rises for new cafes.
Founder payNothing in year 1. From month 13: Rs 1 lakh a month in total once active cafes pass 90; Rs 70,000 until then.
Sales target9 new cafes a month (about 2 a week); 1 in 6 cafes pitched.
Money rulesKeep Rs 2,07,000 in hand before launch plus 3 months of costs as reserve; churn under 3%; review the price after 6 months with real numbers.

The pitch in one line

"Rs 50 a day, less than a coffee. Your card sits in every customer's phone, brings them back, and pays for itself with 8 extra visits a month."

Why not Rs 1,000 or Rs 2,000

  • Rs 1,000 is easy to sell but needs 145 cafes to pay the founders Rs 1 lakh. At 9 a month that is not reached in year 2's first months, so the founders would wait.
  • Rs 2,000 reaches the founders' goal fastest but sits at the edge of what a Rs 2 lakh cafe will spend on one tool, so sign-ups slow and churn rises.
  • Rs 1,500 keeps the founders' goal reachable on a normal selling pace and stays at about half the market leader's price.
